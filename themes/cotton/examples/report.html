    Method and data notes· 3
    1. Resolution time is measured from ticket creation to the final agent reply; 60 tickets per team were sampled for the distribution chart.
    2. CSAT is the share of 4–5 ratings on the post-contact survey, weighted by tickets per team.
    3. Status: on track when SLA ≥ 95% and CSAT ≥ 90%, at risk when SLA < 85% or CSAT < 84%, otherwise watch. See the team overview.
