    How to play

    Drag across a word from its first letter to its last, or tap the first letter and then the last. Use the arrow keys and Enter to do the same from the keyboard, and Esc to cancel. A medium pond also hides words diagonally, and a large one hides some backwards too. Hint makes the first letter of a word ripple.
